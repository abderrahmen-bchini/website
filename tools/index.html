PDF Split

Duplex printing on printers that can't do duplex

Splits any PDF into odd and even page files — with the even pages pre-reversed — so a single-sided printer can print double-sided. Your file never leaves your machine.

The browser edition of my zero-dependency C CLI: it fetches your own machine's info (OS, CPU, GPU, battery…) using standard browser APIs and renders it terminal-style.
